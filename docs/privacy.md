# Privacy boundaries and pending experience policies

This document records engineering boundaries and unresolved approval decisions, not legal advice or an approved retention schedule. The [master V4 blueprint](SIMORA_MASTER_BACKEND_BLUEPRINT_V4.md) is authoritative.

As clarified by the user on 2026-11-06, referral attribution must be consent-aware and first-party. Visitor capture, tracking cookies, device/IP fingerprints, registration attribution and referral financial actions remain disabled. Existing suggested windows, terms and expiry rules require commercial and privacy approval before operation. Demo links do not track visitors.

Reviews, support and optional shared community chat default disabled behind explicit approval gates. New API reads require actual active HTTPS sessions/RBAC; writes additionally require CSRF and verified email. Approval references expire, and current policy references are recorded on content/membership/moderation records. No fixture identity is an authority. Review/community projections use a generic member label and exclude account IDs, names, emails, reports and moderator metadata. Heuristic text checks assist human moderation; they are not comprehensive personal-data detection or a claim of anonymization of free text.

Support conversations are private to their owner and assigned eligible staff. Staff queues and assignment are separately authorized, privacy/security cases require an additional permission, and internal notes are never projected to customers. In-app notifications and outbox entries contain minimal record references/status, not conversation bodies. Audit trails contain actor/action/record/time and bounded reason metadata, never submitted content or authentication tokens. Content remains plain text; future UI consumers must escape it or use textContent.

Optional chat requires named moderation/privacy owners, current policy consent, approved reporting/abuse-response and rights procedures, retention approval and operational staff. Pending/hidden messages are absent from normal snapshots, reporters remain private, bans/mutes apply server-side. Deletion/privacy requests may be submitted as protected `privacy_security` support tickets when the approved support service is enabled. Request intake does not imply automatic erasure, a completion deadline or resolution by an unapproved agent.

Outstanding decisions include notice/consent language, audience, naming, legal basis, minimization review, jurisdiction/age requirements, retention durations, backups/legal holds, erasure/export/anonymization, appeal ownership and support recovery for locked-out users. Proposed 90-day chat retention is not adopted. There is no automatic deletion/retention job or promise of regulatory compliance. Restrictive FKs prevent accidental cascading loss; an approved controlled privacy process must reconcile content/audit retention and any legal holds before launch. TLS ingress, secret management, DB privileges, backups, network abuse limits and operational access review need deployment validation.
